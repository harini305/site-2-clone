(`https://demo.phlox.pro/shop-grocery-2/`, a theme demo by Averta). It does **not** ship any images,
logos or copy from that demo. Those demo assets belong to the theme vendor, and many of the product
photos show third-party trademarked packaging. They are not licensed for reuse on another site.

## Replace before public production use

1. **Brand name and logo.** "Cartly" and the cart-in-a-circle mark are placeholders (`lib/data/site.js`,
   `components/ui/Logo.js`). Replace them with your own brand, and check the name is clear to use.
2. **Contact details.** The address, phone numbers (555 range) and `@cartly.example` emails are placeholders.
3. **Product data.** The prices, product names and descriptions in `lib/data/products.js` are sample content.
4. **Product images (recommended).** The SVG illustrations are production-safe, but real product
   photography will convert better. Keep the same filenames, or update the `images` arrays in
   `lib/data/products.js`.
5. **Pexels photos (optional).** These are fine under the Pexels License. Swap them if you want images
   specific to your store.
